01 About

A multidisciplinary path, connected by engineering discipline.

I am an R&D engineer with a background spanning mechanical engineering, energy and power engineering, microsensors, nanomaterials, and functional thin films. My current focus includes silver nanowire transparent conductive systems, coating and printing processes, conductive inks and pastes, formulation development, material evaluation, and application-oriented R&D.

My work combines hands-on experimentation with process analysis, characterisation, evidence-based troubleshooting, technical documentation, and international project communication. I am especially interested in connecting laboratory research with scalable, clearly validated engineering solutions.
